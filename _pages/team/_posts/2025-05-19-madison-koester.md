Madison Koester is a graduate student at Wichita State University and plans to graduate with an M.S. in Electrical Engineering in Spring 2027. She is currently working on a project involving solver analysis for equivalent circuit model (ECM) parameter estimation using data collected from long-term testing of lithium metal cells. This project evaluates which solver algorithms have the best balance of accuracy and low computational time for the parameter estimation. Outside of academics, Madison enjoys crocheting, hiking, and baking.
